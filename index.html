Direct Advantage

The same apartment, without the platform in the middle.

Booking here goes straight to the people who run the apartments. That changes the price, the flexibility and who answers when you have a question.

01

A better rate, with no platform service fee

We do not pay commission to a booking platform on your behalf, so your rate is lower for the same apartment on the same dates.

02

Direct contact with the host

Ask about parking, late arrival or the best way to reach us and you are talking to the people who run the apartments, not a support queue.

03

Flexibility where we can offer it

Early check-in and late check-out are arranged directly with us and are often possible, subject to the bookings either side of yours.

04

Preferential rates for returning guests

Guests who have stayed before are quoted a returning rate on their next booking. Mention your previous stay when you enquire.

About AK Getaways

An independent serviced-accommodation operator, and the people you deal with directly.

We provide high-quality, fully equipped apartments across a growing number of UK cities, for stays of a night, a week, a month or longer. Every apartment is chosen and finished to a standard we would be happy to stay in ourselves.

We are independent, which means you deal with the host rather than a platform. Booking direct keeps the rate lower and the service personal — the person who answers your enquiry is the person who looks after your stay.

Our commitment is straightforward. Clean, comfortable, well-equipped spaces. Quick, useful replies. And a stay that runs easily from enquiry to check-out, whether you are here for a weekend, working away, or settling in for longer.
